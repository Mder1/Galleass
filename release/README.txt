GALLEASS  v1.0.0
================

A 16th-century Venetian galleass for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord - War Sails: a great war galley with fighting
castles fore and aft, 24 bronze guns, three lateen masts and a spritsail, horn lanterns and a gilded stern lantern.
Entry for the War Sails Modding Competition (3D Art - Ships).

REQUIREMENTS
  Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.5.6 with War Sails (NavalDLC) v1.3.6. No other mods, no code (no DLL).

INSTALL
  Unzip so that the folder "Galleass" sits in  <Bannerlord>/Modules/ , then tick "Galleass" in the launcher
  (it loads after NavalDLC).

GETTING THE SHIP
  Campaign: the galleass is built in the ports of Empire towns - visit a port there and buy it (the price depends
  on the town's market). It has a shallow draft, so it can also take part in naval raids.
  Note: the naval custom battle offers only a fixed list of the game's own ships, so the galleass does not appear
  there - this is the same for every added ship.

HOW IT SAILS
  Heavy and cumbersome in battle: slow under oars and slow to turn, but strong under sail. Fast on the campaign map,
  quicker along coasts and on rivers.
  The guns are part of the ship's look; in this module they do not fire.
