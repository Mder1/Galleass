Adds the Galleass, a 16th-century Venetian war galley, as a new heavy ship to War Sails.

A floating fortress with fighting castles fore and aft, 24 bronze guns along her sides, three lateen masts and a spritsail, and horn lanterns that light up at night. The planking is painted in your faction's colours.

Where to get it:
Purchasable in the ports of Empire towns.
Empire lords can get it for their fleets as well.
Not available in naval custom battle (only the game's own ships are listed there).

The ship:
Heavy class, crew of 112, large cargo hold (4800).
Shallow draft, so it can take part in naval raids and sail on rivers.

Balance: Slow under oars and slow to turn in battle, but strong under sail. Fast on the campaign map.
The guns are decoration in this mod; they do not fire.

Entry for the TaleWorlds War Sails Modding Competition (3D Art - Ships). Made with AI help (code-generated models, no image or 3D generative AI); details in AI_DISCLOSURE.txt. The winged lion on the guns is my own drawing.

Source files (FBX/PNG) are included; the scripts are on GitHub: https://github.com/Mder1/Galleass


Requirements:
Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.5.7 - Older versions are not compatible.
War Sails DLC (NavalDLC) v1.3.7 - Older versions are not compatible.
No other mods needed (no Harmony, no DLL).
