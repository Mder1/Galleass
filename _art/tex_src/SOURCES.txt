lion_drawn.png: the winged lion of the gun reliefs, the author's own pencil drawing (2026-11-12), scanned at
600 dpi, turned upright, contrast raised, specks removed, cropped to the lion and scaled to 3000 px wide.
Cleanup of the scan was script-assisted: faint erased pencil marks on the body removed, the belly line and the
wing's front corner redrawn as smooth curves, one eyebrow moved level with the other.
_art/relief_art.py (lion_relief_drawn) turns it into the relief height field; the founder's initials and the
date are Roman capitals built from strokes in the same script (no font).

Until 2026-11-12 the lion was a public-domain Commons drawing and the lettering Noto Serif; both were
replaced because the 3D Art contest rules allow no derivatives of third-party assets. A lion drawn from own
curves by script (relief_art.lion_relief) followed briefly; the hand drawing replaced it the same day.
